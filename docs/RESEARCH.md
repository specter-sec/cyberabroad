# Read this snapshot carefully

The initial collection contains 30 source-linked postgraduate cybersecurity qualifications, specialisations, or explicitly identified joint pathways. There are three entries for each of the ten requested countries. Sources were reviewed on 15 November 2026; that date does not make historical fees or application cycles current.

**What is known:** 17 records have a sourced annual tuition amount (including labeled annualized estimates); 13 leave it unknown. 13 have a specifically dated, sourced deadline; 17 do not. 19 have a sourced IELTS overall minimum; 11 do not. Numeric IELTS minima are not a full eligibility assessment. None of these counts guarantees an intake is open.

## Course changes worth noticing

Aalto’s old SECCLO option has ended. Its record identifies the active CYBERSURE joint pathway and Aalto second-year specialisation rather than pretending SECCLO is still open. Chalmers’s security specialisation is selected within its current broader Computing Systems master’s, not a separately admitted degree. Several other records are explicitly named security tracks. Read the program and verification notes before treating those routes as equivalent.

UQ’s former Master of Cyber Security was found no longer admitting new students. A generic computer-science substitution was not included in the published dataset; Australia instead includes RMIT’s verified, on-campus Master of Cyber Security. No online-only UNSW degree was mislabeled as a Sydney on-campus option.

## Limits that matter

Fees use the original currency and published academic year. Some official pages lack a reliably attributable annual amount; those values are null rather than guessed. Scholarship eligibility can be narrow, and a funding link does not mean every international applicant qualifies. Deadlines may depend on nationality, visa route, or intake; if no unambiguous full date was established, the date stays null. Past published dates are retained only with their notes and closed presentation in the app.

The site is an independent research tool, not an admissions database operated by the universities. Schema/tests verify structure and data consistency, not whether a live source has changed after review. Every record carries its own sources and caveats. Consult those official pages directly before paying or applying.
